Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Information we collect

The details you send when you contact us, such as your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, travel dates, destination, number of travellers, budget and the information you include in your message.

  • Identity and travel documents you choose to share for bookings or visa application assistance.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare quotations.
  • To arrange the services you confirm, including sharing necessary details with airlines, hotels, tour operators, transfer providers and other suppliers.
  • To help prepare application documents you request.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Sharing

We share personal information only with suppliers and authorities as needed to provide the services you request, or where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above and take reasonable steps to protect it.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting info@bygoodnesstravels.com.

Website

This website does not use advertising cookies. When you send an enquiry, it opens in WhatsApp or your email app, and those services apply their own privacy terms.
