Questions

Frequently asked

More answers on our FAQ page.

Can I request a customised travel package?

Yes. You can tell us your preferred destination, dates, number of travellers, budget and the services you would like included. We can then discuss suitable available options.

Can you assist with visa applications?

We provide application guidance and document preparation assistance for selected destinations. Eligibility and final decisions rest with the relevant immigration authorities.

Are flights and hotels included in every package?

No. Inclusions vary by package. Please review the quotation and written booking confirmation to understand what is included and excluded.
