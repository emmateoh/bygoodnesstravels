Booking & Cancellation Policy

How bookings, payments, changes and cancellations work.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Making a booking

A booking is confirmed only after you accept the quotation, the applicable terms and payment conditions, and we issue a written confirmation.

Payments

Payment amounts, deadlines and accepted methods are stated in your quotation. Some suppliers require full payment at the time of booking.

Changes

Change requests depend on supplier rules and availability. Supplier change fees and fare differences may apply, as well as our service fee where stated.

Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation conditions are set by each supplier and are explained before you confirm. Refunds, where available, follow the supplier’s rules and timelines. Service fees for work already completed, such as visa application preparation, may be non-refundable where stated.

Travel insurance

We recommend that you take out suitable travel insurance when you book.

Contact

To change or cancel a booking, contact us by WhatsApp +974 3099 5071 or email info@bygoodnesstravels.com.
